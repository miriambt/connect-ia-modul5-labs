Norvik Logistics Expenses Policy v3.1, effective 2026-02-01. This policy covers business expenses paid by employees and later reimbursed by the company. Expenses must be submitted through the expense app within 30 days of the date they were incurred. Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days late are reimbursed only with an exception approved by both the manager and the Finance Controller. Claims older than 60 days are rejected without exception. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll if approved before the 20th of the month; otherwise, with the following payroll. The employee's band on the date the expense was incurred determines which limits apply.

Each expense report must be approved according to its total amount. Reports up to 500 euros need the manager's approval only. Reports between 500.01 and 2,000 euros need the manager and the head of department. Reports above 2,000 euros additionally need the Chief Financial Officer. Splitting one expense into several reports to stay below a threshold is considered a fraudulent claim. Band E employees never approve their own expenses: all their reports, regardless of amount, are approved by the CFO, and the CFO's own expenses are approved by the CEO.

Flights must be booked through the corporate travel agency. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or more, Bands C, D and E may book premium economy. Business class is allowed only for flights of 9 hours or more, and only for Band E or with prior written approval from the CFO. Booking outside the agency is allowed only if the fare is at least 15 percent cheaper than the agency's offer for the same route and date, and a screenshot of the agency's offer must be attached. Seat selection and one checked bag are reimbursable; priority boarding and lounge access are not.

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to a nightly cap that depends on the city. The caps are 140 euros in Barcelona and Lisbon, 160 euros in Madrid, 220 euros in London and Paris, 150 euros in any other EU city and 180 euros outside the EU. Employees in Bands D and E have caps 20 percent higher. The caps exclude tourist taxes, which are reimbursed in full on top of the cap. If the room costs more than the cap, the employee pays the difference, unless the hotel was booked by the travel agency because no cheaper option was available, which the agency confirms by email.

When travelling, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 55 euros for domestic trips and 70 euros for international trips, without needing meal receipts. If breakfast is included in the hotel rate, the daily allowance is reduced by 15 percent. Travel days count as full days if departure is before 14:00 or return after 20:00; otherwise they count as half days. Client entertainment is not covered by the daily allowance: it is claimed separately, requires the names and companies of all attendees, and is capped at 90 euros per person. Alcohol may not exceed 30 percent of the total bill; any excess is not reimbursed.

Use of a personal car for business trips is reimbursed at 0.26 euros per kilometre, plus tolls and parking with receipts. Commuting between home and the usual office is never reimbursed. Taxis are reimbursed when public transport would take more than 45 minutes longer, when travelling with heavy work equipment, or for any trip between 22:00 and 06:00. Rideshare apps are allowed under the same conditions, but only standard tiers; premium tiers are not reimbursed. Car rental requires the manager's prior approval and must be the smallest category suitable for the number of passengers.

The following are never reimbursed: personal software subscriptions, even when used for work, because software must be requested through IT; home internet costs, because they are covered by the HR internet stipend paid through payroll; home office furniture, which is covered by the HR home office allowance; traffic and parking fines; hotel minibar items; in-flight purchases; personal travel insurance; upgrades of any kind paid by the employee; and expenses of accompanying family members. Laundry is reimbursed only for business trips lasting longer than 5 consecutive nights.

Every expense must have an itemised receipt. For any expense above 75 euros, a full VAT invoice in the name of Norvik Logistics S.L. with the company tax number is required; a simple receipt is not enough. Expenses in foreign currency are converted at the rate shown on the card statement when paid by card, or at the European Central Bank reference rate of the transaction date when paid in cash. Lost receipts can be replaced by a signed declaration no more than twice per calendar year and only for expenses up to 50 euros each.

Employees in Band C or higher receive a corporate card with a monthly limit of 3,000 euros, which can be raised to 6,000 euros by the CFO for a specific trip. Lower bands may request a card if they travel more than 6 times per year. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited. An accidental personal charge must be reported and repaid within 10 days; a second accidental personal charge within 12 months results in the card being cancelled. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the expense app by the 5th business day of the following month.

Finance audits a random 5 percent of expense reports every month, plus every report above 2,000 euros. Fraudulent claims, including split reports and falsified receipts, are treated as serious breaches under the disciplinary procedure of the HR Policy, and the amounts are recovered from payroll. Because limits depend on the band on the date of the expense, an employee promoted to a higher band can use the new limits from the date the promotion takes effect. Questions about this policy go to the Finance Controller. Version 3.1 replaces version 3.0 and introduced the half-day rule for travel days.
