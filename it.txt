Norvik Logistics IT Policy v4.2, effective 2026-01-01. The IT Service Desk classifies every ticket by priority. P1 (company-wide outage or security breach) has a 15-minute response time and a 4-hour resolution target. P2 (a whole team blocked) has a 1-hour response and an 8 business-hour resolution target. P3 (one person blocked, workaround available) has a 4-hour response and a 3 business-day target. P4 (requests and questions) is answered the next business day and resolved within 10 business days. Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays of the Barcelona office calendar. This applies to Lisbon staff too, so a P3 opened in Lisbon on a Barcelona holiday starts counting on the next Barcelona business day.

Laptops are allocated according to the employee's HR band, defined in the HR Policy. Bands A and B receive the standard 14-inch model with 16 GB of RAM. Bands C and D receive the performance 16-inch model with 32 GB of RAM. Band E receives the executive ultralight model. Contractors receive a standard 14-inch model unless their contract states otherwise. The refresh cycle is 36 months from the delivery date, except for members of the Data Engineering team, whose cycle is 30 months. Early replacement is only approved when the quoted repair cost exceeds 40 percent of the device's residual value. A band change after a promotion does not trigger an immediate laptop upgrade; the new entitlement applies at the next scheduled refresh.

Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Since March 2024 there is no periodic password rotation: a password is changed only when compromise is suspected or confirmed. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account and must use the approved authenticator app. SMS codes are tolerated only for warehouse staff who have no company phone, and only until 2027-06-30, after which hardware security keys will be issued to them. Shared accounts are forbidden, with the single exception of the warehouse label printers, which use service accounts managed by IT. Writing passwords on paper or storing them in browsers is not allowed; the corporate password manager is the only approved storage.

Remote access uses an always-on VPN client installed on every company laptop. Split tunneling is disabled, except for the domains of the approved video conferencing platform, which bypass the VPN to improve call quality. Connecting from inside the European Union requires no notification. Connecting from outside the EU requires a travel notification through the IT portal form TRV-2 submitted at least 5 business days before departure. Without that notification, the VPN gateway blocks logins from non-EU locations automatically. Countries listed in the current quarterly Security Advisory are blocked even with a TRV-2 notification; travel to those countries is covered by the loaner laptop procedure described later in this policy.

Software listed in the IT catalogue is installed automatically after the request, with no approval. Software outside the catalogue needs approval from the employee's manager and a security review, which takes up to 5 business days. Any SaaS tool that will process personal data also requires sign-off from the Data Protection Officer, which adds up to 10 business days. Licences costing up to 200 euros per user per year are charged to the requesting department's cost centre. More expensive licences are charged to the central IT budget only when at least 10 users need them; otherwise the department pays. Software bought personally by an employee is never reimbursed through expense reports, even if it is used for work.

Every company laptop must sync the Documents and Desktop folders to the corporate cloud drive. Files stored only locally are not backed up, and IT is not responsible for their loss. Deleted cloud files can be recovered for 90 days. Mailboxes are retained for 7 years for Finance staff and 3 years for everyone else. When an employee leaves, their account is disabled at 18:00 CET on their last working day and permanently deleted 30 days later. The only exception is a legal hold requested by the Legal department, in which case the account and mailbox are preserved until Legal lifts the hold, regardless of the standard retention periods.

Security incidents, including phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported to the Security Desk on internal extension 4400 within 1 hour of being noticed. A lost or stolen device must be reported within 2 hours so it can be remotely wiped. If a lost device is reported more than 24 hours after the loss, the employee pays a 150-euro deductible. If the same employee loses a second device within 24 months of the first loss, they pay the full residual value of the second device, regardless of how quickly it was reported, and the case is forwarded to HR under the disciplinary procedure.

Peripherals such as keyboards, mice, headsets and docking stations are ordered through the IT catalogue and delivered to the office or to the employee's home. IT provides one external monitor per employee. A second monitor is provided only to employees in Band C or higher and to Finance analysts of any band. Chairs, desks and other home office furniture are not IT equipment; they are covered by the home office allowance managed by HR, and IT will not process those requests. Broken peripherals are replaced free of charge once per year per item; further replacements of the same item within 12 months are charged to the department.

Employees travelling to a country listed in the current Security Advisory (SA-2026-Q4 lists seven countries, published on the IT portal) must not take their regular laptop or phone. Instead, they request a loaner travel laptop and a loaner phone from the IT pool at least 10 business days before departure. Loaner devices have no cached credentials and only access email and the document portal through a browser. They must be returned within 3 business days after returning from the trip, and they are fully wiped before reuse. If no loaner is available, the trip must be postponed or the work done without company devices.

Exceptions to this policy must be approved in writing by the Chief Information Security Officer and are valid for a maximum of 6 months, after which they must be renewed. Where this policy conflicts with local law, local law prevails. Where it conflicts with the HR Policy, the HR Policy prevails for employment matters such as working hours, disciplinary measures and allowances, while this IT Policy prevails for technical matters such as devices, accounts and access. Questions about this policy can be raised as P4 tickets. This version 4.2 replaces version 4.1 and was approved by the IT Steering Committee in November 2025.
